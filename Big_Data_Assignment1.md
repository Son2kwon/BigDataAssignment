# Project Proposal

First step toward the final data analysis project.

### Objective

- Define a concrete data-driven problem and explain why it is worth solving.
- Choose an appropriate analysis method and describe how it will be applied to the problem.

### Scope

- Any data analysis technique is allowed: classical ML, CNN, LSTM, Transformer, GNN, reinforcement learning, etc.
- Public datasets such as Kaggle are allowed; other accessible datasets may also be used.

# What to Include

The proposal should explain what you will solve, how you will solve it, and why.

### Problem & Motivation

- What problem will you address?
- Why is the problem meaningful or interesting?

### Data

- Dataset source, approximate size, main variables or features, and prediction / analysis target.

### Method & Evaluation

- Planned model or analysis method and the reason it is suitable for the problem.
- Planned evaluation metrics and, when applicable, the validation strategy.

### Expected Outcome

- What result, insight, or practical value do you expect the project to demonstrate?

# Individual or Team of Two

The proposal is individual by default, but collaboration is encouraged.

### Submission

- Submit individually unless a two-person team has already been agreed in advance.
- A team is limited to two students.

### Team Policy

- Both members must contribute equally to the project.
- Both members receive the same project grade.
- A small collaboration bonus will be applied to two-person team projects.
- The project scope and workload should be appropriate for two people.

# Topic Sharing & Team Formation

Individual proposals can be used to find classmates with similar project interests.

### After Proposal Submission

- The instructor will review individual proposals and group similar project topics.
- A brief summary will be posted on the class board by topic.
- Only essential information will be shared: student name, project topic, data type, and planned method.

### Forming a Team

- Students with similar interests may contact each other and form a team of two.
- After forming a team, members may select one idea, merge two ideas, or revise the project scope.
- Individual projects remain fully allowed.
